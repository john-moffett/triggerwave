Tortoise Support

Help with Tortoise for iPhone

Contact Us

For help, to report someone, or to ask us to delete your Friends data, email tortoisesupport@triggerwave.com and we'll get back to you. Please tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened.

Adding a Friend

Friends arrives in a coming update to the App. Until then there is no Friends tab; this and the next two sections are how it will work.

Friends is optional, and it needs iCloud turned on for your iPhone. In the App, open the Friends tab.

  • To invite someone: tap Invite someone, choose a welcome gift (or none), and send the message the App writes. It has an 8-character code, like K7Q2-M9XF, and the link to the App. A code works for one friend, the first person to use it, and lasts 14 days. You can have up to 5 open invites, and you can cancel one.
  • To use a friend's code: open the Friends tab and tap I have a code, then paste it (or type it). New to the App? The setup asks "How did you hear about me?" and, if you pick a friend or family, "Got an invite?"
  • The first time, the App asks which name your friends will see and asks you to say "I agree" to the rules in the Terms of Use.

Friends only ever see the name you chose for them, your tortoise's name, its growth stage and outfit, and the gifts you gave them that they kept. They never see your moves, steps, Health data or trips, or when you were last in the App.

Sending a Gift or a Wave

Tap a friend's card to go to their trailhead. Send a gift lets you pick a spare souvenir, a copy of a postcard, or something from the shop. Wave sends one of the preset lines; there is no free text. You can send each friend one gift and one wave a day. Your tortoise carries a gift, and carrying costs a little clover, unless you have Plus or it goes with an invite. If a gift can't be delivered, it comes back to you with your clover.

Removing, Blocking or Reporting a Friend

Open a friend's card and tap the menu.

  • Remove takes them off your trail. Their gifts stay on your shelf, and they aren't told. They drop off their list the next time their phone updates.
  • Block does the same, and they can't use your invites again; anything from them is ignored.
  • Report is for a rude or hurtful name, someone you don't know, or something else. It blocks them at once and sends us the report. We look at reports within 24 hours, and may hide the name or remove the person from Friends.

The people you've blocked are listed in Settings, under Friends.

Restoring Your Purchase

In the App, open Settings, find the Plus row and tap Restore. (It's also on the offer screen.) Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. If Plus still doesn't show, wait a minute, reopen the App and try again, then email us.

Cancelling Plus

You cancel in your App Store settings, not in the App. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Tortoise Plus and tap Cancel. Do it at least 24 hours before the period ends, or it renews. Deleting the App doesn't cancel it. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com: Apple handles refunds.

The Free-Week Reminder

If you start the 7-day free trial and allow notifications, the App sends you a reminder on day 5, before the trial turns into a paid subscription. It's a local reminder, so it only works if notifications are on. If you turned them off, set your own reminder to cancel in App Store settings. The yearly plan then starts after the 7 days, unless you cancel first.

Moving to a New Phone

Your tortoise and progress are saved on your iPhone only. There is no account, and the App doesn't sync them through iCloud or anywhere else. If you restore your new phone from an iPhone backup (iCloud Backup, or a backup on your computer), the App's saved data normally comes across with it. If you set up the new phone as new, you'd start again with a new egg. Plus comes with your Apple Account: tap Restore on the new phone.

Steps and Apple Health

The App can read your step count for today, so your steps count toward trips. To change this, open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Tortoise and switch Steps on or off. Steps go only to the App on your phone. If you'd rather not share them, you can log moves by hand.

Notifications

Turn reminders on or off in Settings → Notifications → Tortoise. Reminders are local: your phone makes them, and nothing is sent from a server. Notices about friends (a gift, a wave, or someone joining through your invite) come through Apple's push service. You can turn those off in the App under Settings → Friends → Friend notifications. Friends still works with notifications off. If you aren't getting them, check that Focus modes or Scheduled Summary aren't hiding them.

Deleting Your Data

Everything you've done in the App is on your phone, so deleting the App deletes it, except what Friends keeps (see below): press and hold the icon and choose Remove App, then Delete App. This can't be undone. We hold no account for you to close. Deleting the App doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel it too (see above).

If you used Friends: deleting the App does not remove your friend card or invites from iCloud. Open Settings → Friends → Remove me first. It takes your friend card, invites and everything you've sent off our servers straight away, and removes your friends from this phone. Your tortoise and everything on the shelf stay right here. Your friends' phones drop you at their next update, and gifts you gave stay on their shelves. If you've already deleted the App, reinstall it and use Remove me, or email us and we'll delete it for you.
