Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 (the App was called Koi Falls until November 20, 2026)

TriggerWave LLC ("Triggerwave", "we", "us") makes the app Pocket Falls (the "App") for iPhone and iPad. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short, the App collects nothing about you.

1. What We Collect

Nothing. The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet. We don't receive your name, email address, contacts, location, usage or any other information about you or your device from the App.

2. What Stays on Your Device

The App remembers your settings on your device so they're there next time: the scene, the sounds, how many koi, night mode, the sleep timer and the time of any alarm you set. They never leave your device, and deleting the App deletes them.

3. Alarms

If you set an alarm, the App schedules it with Apple's AlarmKit, which keeps it on your device so it can ring even when your phone is locked. iOS asks your permission the first time you set one. You can change or remove that permission at any time in the Settings app.

4. Motion and Sound

The App can use your device's motion sensors so the scene shifts a little as you tilt it. The readings are used on the device as you move it and are neither kept nor sent anywhere. You can turn this off with the Parallax switch in the App's settings. The App plays its sounds on your device and never uses the microphone.

5. Purchases

If the App offers an optional purchase, it is made through the App Store and handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple only tells the App whether a purchase is active, so it can unlock what you bought.

6. Beta Testing

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us what its TestFlight terms describe. That can include the email address you were invited with (testers who join by a public link stay anonymous), your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve the App.

7. Children

The App doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Your Rights

Since we hold no personal data from the App, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For beta feedback you sent us through TestFlight, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the App changes what it does with data, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@triggerwave.com. Help with the App, or feedback: pocketfallssupport@triggerwave.com.
