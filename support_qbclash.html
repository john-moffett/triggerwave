Quarterback Clash Support

Help with Quarterback Clash (QB Clash) for iPhone, iPad and Android

Contact Us

Email qbclashsupport@triggerwave.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your player name and team, your device and what happened.

Help in the Game

Tap the ? at the top of the Play screen:

  • How to play: the four training throws again, in about a minute.
  • Report a play: if something in a play looked wrong. It sends us your last few plays, so we can watch them back.
  • Report a name: pick the rival whose name you found offensive. They won't know it was you, and they're blocked too unless you untick the box.
  • Block a player: never be matched with them again. They won't know.
  • Send feedback: bugs, ideas, anything you'd change.

Your Name

You choose a name after the training throws, and can change it on your team screen: the first change is free. Names are checked against our community rules (in the Game: Help, then Rules and privacy). If one says Name not available, it didn't pass: please try another. A name that breaks the rules is taken away and your team's name is shown instead.

Purchases

  • What you buy is added once the App Store or Google Play has confirmed your purchase. If it doesn't appear straight away, stay online and reopen the Game. If it's still missing, email us with your player name and the store's receipt or order number.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, and by Google Play: see Google Play's refund policies.

Analytics

To turn analytics on or off, open the Game's settings. Our privacy policy explains what is sent.

Deleting Your Data

Email qbclashsupport@triggerwave.com with your player name and team so we can find your game. We'll delete your game, your duel recordings and your reports from our server. Deleting the app on its own doesn't delete them.

Records of purchases are kept under an anonymous identifier as financial records and so nothing is delivered twice. Our server's logs are deleted after 30 days. The privacy policy has the details.
