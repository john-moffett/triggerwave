Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Triggerwave ("we", "us", "our") makes mobile games (each a "Game"). This policy applies to every Triggerwave Game that links to it. A Game that uses third-party services, such as advertising or analytics, has its own privacy policy, linked from its store listing, and that policy applies instead.

1. Data We Collect

None. The Games covered by this policy do not collect personal information, do not require an account or login, and contain no advertising, analytics or tracking.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress and settings are saved locally on your device. They are never transmitted to us, and deleting a Game deletes them.

3. In-App Purchases

If a Game offers in-app purchases, they are processed entirely by the platform store (the Apple App Store or Google Play). We do not receive or store any payment information.

4. Apple and Google

Apple and Google may share anonymous crash reports and app analytics with us if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings. If you test a Game through TestFlight, Apple may also share crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use this information only to fix problems and improve our Games.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the Games covered by this policy collect no personal information, they collect none from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
