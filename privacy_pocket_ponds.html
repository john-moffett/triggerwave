Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Triggerwave ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile game Pocket Ponds (the "Game"). This page describes what data is collected when you use the Game, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It covers Pocket Ponds 1.2 and later for iPhone and iPad; section 11 covers earlier versions.

1. Data We Collect

Pocket Ponds keeps your ponds on our server, so your game is saved and your friends can visit. The Game creates an account for you the first time you play. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location, and it has no ads, analytics or tracking.

To run your account, our server stores:

  • Your account ID and username: an identifier the Game creates for your account, and the username you choose. Used to save your game and so friends can find and visit you.
  • Your game: your ponds, koi and decorations and the names you give them; your koins, credits, level and awards; your friends list; likes, visits, gifts and in-game mail between you and other players; and your Koi Log of the breeds you've owned. Used to save and load your game and for visits between players.
  • Purchase history: which packs you bought, with the App Store's transaction IDs and dates. Used to deliver each purchase exactly once, and to add any that weren't delivered.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, the token Apple gives the Game for your device. Used to tell you when someone likes your pond or sends you a koi.

2. How We Use It

Only to run the Game: to save your ponds, deliver what you buy, let friends find and visit you, show featured and random ponds, and send the notifications you allow. We don't use it for advertising, we don't build profiles from it, and we don't sell it.

3. What Other Players See

When other players visit your pond, they can see your username, your ponds, your koi and the names you give them. Your pond may also appear among featured and random ponds, where usernames are partly hidden. Friends add each other by username. Please don't use your real name or other personal details in your username or in pond and koi names.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game keeps a copy of your game and your settings on your device. Your sign-in is kept in the device's keychain, so it moves to a new iPhone or iPad when you restore it from a backup. Reminders, such as when your koi are hungry, are scheduled on the device. Pond photos are saved to your photo library or shared only when you choose to, and are never sent to us.

5. Third-Party Services

We share data only with these service providers, which process it to run the Game, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Amazon Web Services: hosts our server and its database, which hold the data in section 1. AWS Privacy Notice
  • Apple: processes in-app purchases and delivers push notifications. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies: the Game is built with Unity and handles purchases with Unity In-App Purchasing, which sends Unity technical diagnostics about the purchasing library itself, such as errors and how long store requests take. It does not send your account, username or payment details. Unity's analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting are switched off. Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our players and ourselves.

6. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the App Store, under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We receive confirmation of what was bought, with its transaction ID and date, never your payment information.

7. Apple

Apple may share anonymous crash reports and app analytics with us if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings. If you test the Game through TestFlight, Apple may also share crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use this information only to fix problems and improve the Game.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game doesn't ask any player, of any age, for personal information such as a name, email address or location, and it has no ads or tracking. Other players see usernames and pond and koi names, so please help children choose ones that don't identify them. A parent or guardian can contact us to review or delete a child's account.

9. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • We keep your account's data while your account exists.
  • To delete your account, open Visit Ponds in the Game, tap your username and choose Delete account. This removes your ponds, koi, decorations, friends, likes, visits, gifts, in-game mail, Koi Log and username from our server, and the device forgets your sign-in and its copy of your game.
  • Records of purchases, and of koi sent as gifts, are kept after deletion under an anonymous identifier, as financial records and so that nothing is delivered twice.
  • Our server's logs, which we use to find and fix problems, are deleted after 30 days.
  • You can also ask us to delete your account by email. Tell us your username so we can find it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, and to object to or restrict its use. Contact us to make a request, with your username so we can find your account.

Where the GDPR applies, we process your account's data to provide the Game you chose to play, and keep logs in our legitimate interest in keeping it working and secure.

11. Earlier Versions

Versions of Pocket Ponds before 1.2, which some older iPhones and iPads still run, also let you sign in with Facebook to find friends, through Facebook's software in the Game and under Meta's Privacy Policy. Version 1.2 removed Facebook. If you signed in with Facebook in an earlier version, our server may still hold your Facebook user ID, linked to your account; it is deleted with your account.

12. Security and International Transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Amazon Web Services in the United States.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
