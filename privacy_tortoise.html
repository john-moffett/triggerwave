Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

TriggerWave LLC ("Triggerwave", "we", "us", "our") makes Tortoise, a movement app for iPhone with a small cartoon tortoise for company (the "App"). This page explains what the App collects, why, who else sees it, and the choices you have. The short version: your progress stays on your phone, and there is no account. If you choose to use Friends, a few things travel through iCloud so your friends can see them (section 4), and your iCloud account identifies you to Friends and nowhere else.

1. What Stays on Your Phone

The App has no account and no sign-in of our own. If you use Friends, your iCloud account identifies you to it (section 4); otherwise nothing about you is tied to an account. We never ask for your email address, phone number or contacts, and we don't collect your location.

These are saved on your iPhone only:

  • your first name and your tortoise's name (if you use Friends, a name you choose for friends and your tortoise's name are also shared as described in section 4);
  • your answers to the questions at the start (what you hope for, what you like, what gets in the way, how much you can do);
  • the moves you log, your trips, weeks, finds and souvenirs;
  • your settings, and your reminder times.

We don't receive any of this as you wrote it. Your first name, your tortoise's name, the names of your own activities and your answers to the questions at the start never leave your phone, except the few Friends items in section 4. The App does send anonymous counts of what happens in it, such as that a move from its list was logged and how big it was, or that a trip came home (section 6). Deleting the App deletes what is on your phone (see section 9). A small summary of your tortoise is also saved in a shared space on your phone so the widget can show it. It never leaves the phone either.

2. Apple Health

If you say yes, the App reads one thing from Apple Health: your step count for today, so your steps can count toward trips. It is read-only. The App doesn't write anything to Health, and it doesn't read anything else from it.

Your step count is used on the phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is not sent to analytics or advertising services, and we don't use Health data for marketing, advertising or data mining. Our analytics (section 6) can note that you turned your steps into paces, but never how many steps, or anything else from Health. You can turn access off at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Tortoise. The App works without it: you can log moves by hand.

3. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications. Your phone schedules them itself. If you use Friends, notices that a friend sent a gift or a wave, or that someone you invited has joined, arrive through Apple's push service via iCloud. We still run no push server and no device token is sent to us. The wording of each notice is written by your phone, never copied from what a friend sent. They are optional: if you say no, everything still works. You can change this in Settings → Notifications.

4. Friends

Friends is optional. Nothing in this section applies unless you make or use an invite. Friends needs an iCloud account on your phone; the App can't use it without one.

What is stored. When you use Friends, these are saved in the App's iCloud database:

  • your friend card: the name you chose for friends, your tortoise's name, its growth stage and its outfit;
  • invites you make, with the gift you packed, and the invite codes you use;
  • gifts and waves you send: which item, which line, and when;
  • a backup of your friends list, which only you can read;
  • reports you file about someone.

Where. In Apple's CloudKit, in the App's own iCloud database. Apple runs it. We can see what is in it.

Who sees what. Only the friends you add can see your friend card, and only you can read your backup. Your friends see the gifts you send them and the waves; a friend's list of who else they know is not visible to you, and yours is not visible to them. A report is read by you and by us.

What is never shared. Your moves, steps, Apple Health data, trips, streaks, how often you open the App, and your answers to the questions at the start are never sent to Friends or to your friends, and nothing in our analytics carries your iCloud ID, your friends' names or invite codes. (Anonymous counts of how the App is used go to our analytics service; see section 6.)

How long we keep each thing.

  • waves: 7 days;
  • gifts: until your friend keeps them, and at most 90 days;
  • invites: 14 days;
  • your friend card and your backup: until you use Remove me;
  • reports: until we have handled them, and at most 90 days.

Deleting it. Open Settings in the App, go to Friends and tap Remove me. It deletes your friend card, your invites and everything you have sent from the iCloud database straight away, and your friends' phones drop you the next time they update. Gifts you gave stay with the friends who kept them, because they are saved on their phones. If we have banned someone from Friends, we keep a short note of the ban so it holds. Deleting the App does not remove your Friends data: reinstall and use Remove me, or write to us (section 13).

The iCloud ID. Apple gives the App an ID for your iCloud account that works in this App only. It isn't your email address or your name, and it is not the same in other apps. We use it to know whose records are whose.

Friends is not for children under 13 (see section 8).

5. Purchases

Tortoise Plus is an optional subscription, bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment and gives the App confirmation that you subscribed, so it can unlock Plus. We never see your card or payment details, or your Apple Account details. The App checks your subscription on your phone, using Apple's StoreKit. See our Terms of Use for the subscription terms.

6. Analytics and Measurement

The App uses TelemetryDeck, an analytics service run by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany, to count how the App is used, so we can fix confusing screens and see what helps people keep moving. When something happens in the App, such as opening it, finishing a step of the setup, logging a move, sending your tortoise on a trip, opening a homecoming or seeing an offer, the App sends TelemetryDeck a short event: what happened; a few details about it, such as which move from the App's own list it was and how big, where the trip went, or which test version of the offer your install was given; how many days ago you installed the App; and basic facts about the phone and the App, such as its model, iOS version, App version, language and region.

Each event carries an anonymous ID for your copy of the App. It is made from an ID Apple gives the App on your phone, scrambled on your phone and scrambled again by TelemetryDeck. It doesn't identify you, and it isn't your Apple Account or your phone's advertising identifier. Events never contain your name, your tortoise's name, the names of your own activities or anything else you type, your step count or anything else from Apple Health, invite codes, or your iCloud ID: the App checks every event for these before it leaves your phone. There is no advertising identifier and no tracking. TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy, TelemetryDeck privacy FAQ.

Testing through TestFlight. In some test versions, after a week the App asks once what you did that week because of the tortoise. If you answer, your answer is sent to us through TelemetryDeck, with your name and your tortoise's name taken out. You can skip it. The version on the App Store never asks.

Apple Ads. If you installed the App after tapping one of our ads on the App Store, the App asks Apple, through Apple’s AdServices framework, whether an ad brought you and, if so, which campaign, ad group, keyword and ad. Apple answers with those numbers only, never who you are. The App adds them to its analytics events (above), so we can tell which ads bring people who enjoy the App. This needs no tracking permission and uses no advertising identifier. Apple Privacy Policy

7. Who Else May See Anything

  • Apple: the App Store handles purchases. If you have chosen to share crash reports and app analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous reports. If you test through TestFlight, Apple may also share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send. We use these only to fix problems. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit): if you use Friends, Apple stores the items in section 4 for us and delivers the notices described in section 3. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Rive: the animation software that moves the tortoise runs inside the App. It doesn't send anything anywhere.
  • TelemetryDeck: receives the anonymous usage events described in section 6, to count how the App is used. TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy

We don't sell your information. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and ourselves.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Friends is not for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used the App and want any information about them deleted, please contact us.

9. Keeping and Deleting Data

  • On your phone: deleting the App deletes your tortoise, your logs and your settings. They can't be recovered by us.
  • With Apple: your purchase history belongs to your Apple Account. Manage it in your Apple Account settings.
  • Friends: use Remove me in Settings, under Friends, to delete what we hold about you in the iCloud database (section 4). Deleting the App alone does not do this. If you can't use the App, write to us at the address below and we will delete it for you.
  • Analytics: the events in section 6 are anonymous, so we can't pick out yours to show you or delete them. TelemetryDeck stores them for us, and we keep them only while they help us improve the App.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or California law), you may have the right to see, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to make a request. Because the App has no accounts of its own, we may need your install ID to find anything we hold. For Friends, the quickest way to delete your data is Remove me in Settings (section 4); if you can't use it, write to us and we will find your records through your iCloud ID for this App. Since your progress is only on your phone, you already hold nearly all of it.

11. Security and International Transfers

Data we receive is sent over encrypted connections. Our service providers may process it in the United States and other countries, with the safeguards those providers put in place.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
