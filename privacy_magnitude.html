Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Triggerwave ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile game Magnitude (the "Game"). This page describes what data is collected when you use the Game and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

None. The Game does not collect personal information, does not require an account or login, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. It makes no network connections of its own.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress (levels beaten, stars and best scores) and your settings (sound, graphics and haptics) are saved locally on your device. They are never transmitted to us, and deleting the Game deletes them.

3. In-App Purchases

If the Game offers in-app purchases, they are processed entirely by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information.

4. Apple

Apple may share anonymous crash reports and app analytics with us if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings. If you test the Game through TestFlight, Apple may also share crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use this information only to fix problems and improve the Game.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the Game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
