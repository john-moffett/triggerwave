Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Triggerwave ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile game Magnitude Kaiju (the "Game"). This page describes what data is collected when you use the Game, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and we do not collect your precise location. The Game does not require an account or login.

The following data is collected automatically while you play, through the third-party services described below:

  • Gameplay events: levels started and finished, scores (the magnitude reached), stars, how long runs last, revives, skin trials, shop visits, and when and how often you play. Used to understand how the Game is played and to improve it. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics identifiers are only stored on your device if you consent (see section 4a).
  • Crash and performance data: crash reports and error logs, with the device model, operating system and app version, and the level being played at the time. Used to find and fix bugs.
  • Device and app identifiers: identifiers assigned to the app installation by our analytics and crash reporting services, and your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking). Used to count players and sessions, and for advertising.
  • Approximate location: your country or region, inferred from your IP address. Used for analytics and to show ads suited to your region.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, watched or tapped, and ad revenue. Used to show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress (levels beaten, stars and best scores) and your settings (sound, graphics and haptics) are saved locally on your device. They are never transmitted to us, and deleting the Game deletes them.

3. Leaderboards

The daily cities have leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android), your daily scores are sent to that service and appear on its leaderboards with your Game Center or Play Games name, where other players can see them. Signing in is optional and handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

4. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads, served through Unity LevelPlay, which chooses ads from advertising networks including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. These services receive the device information, advertising identifier, approximate location and ad interactions described above, to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize them.

  • iPhone: the advertising identifier is only available if you allow tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before ads start (see section 4a). If you don't consent, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalized.

4a. Your Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

When you first open the Game in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, it asks for your consent, using Google's consent tool, before the Game or our advertising partners store or access information on your device for personalized ads, ad measurement or analytics. You can consent, decline, or choose by purpose and by partner. Your choice is stored on your device and passed to our advertising partners in the standard IAB Transparency & Consent Framework format.

You can change your choice at any time in the Game: open Settings and tap Privacy choices.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only with these service providers, which process it to run the features above, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google: Google Analytics for Firebase (gameplay analytics), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports), Google Play Games (Android leaderboards) and Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform, which asks for consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Unity Technologies: Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation), ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Apple: Game Center (iPhone leaderboards). Apple Privacy Policy

Under some laws (such as California's), sharing information with advertising partners for personalized ads can count as "selling" or "sharing". You can opt out at any time with your device's ad settings described above. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our players and ourselves.

6. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation of what was bought, never your payment information.

7. Apple

Apple may share anonymous crash reports and app analytics with us if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings. If you test the Game through TestFlight, Apple may also share crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use this information only to fix problems and improve the Game.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game and you would like their information deleted, please contact us.

9. Data Retention

  • Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies, linked above.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to make a request; because the Game has no accounts, we may need an identifier from your device to find your data. Uninstalling the Game stops all collection.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for analytics and personalized ads, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the Game stable and secure for crash reports.

11. Security and International Transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our service providers may process it in the United States and other countries, with the safeguards those providers put in place.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
