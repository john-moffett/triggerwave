Help with Pocket Falls for iPhone and iPad

Contact Us

For help or feedback, email pocketfallssupport@triggerwave.com and we'll get back to you. Please tell us your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version and what happened.

Seeing the Waterfall

  • iPhone Duo: fold the phone halfway, like a laptop. The waterfall runs from the upper screen into the pond on the lower screen. The more you fold it, the stronger and louder the waterfall. Open it flat and the waterfall becomes a stream.
  • Other iPhones and iPad: tap the water, then tap the round button that appears in the lower right corner, and turn on Waterfall.

Settings

Tap the water, then the round button in the lower right corner. There you can choose a scene (granite, slate or sandstone), how many koi are in the pond (none to eight), the plants, whether the sunlight moves as you tilt the phone, sound effects and ambient sound, night mode, the sleep timer and the alarm.

Sound

Pocket Falls plays its sound even when your iPhone's silent switch or mode is on. To make it quieter, use the volume buttons, or turn off Sound effects or Ambience in the settings. The Sleep timer fades the sound out after 15 minutes, 30 minutes, an hour or two hours.

Night Mode and the Alarm

Night mode dims the pond and shows the time. While night mode or the sleep timer is on, the sound keeps playing with the screen locked.

The alarm plays the pond's birdsong at the time you choose, even with your iPhone locked. The first time you set one, iOS asks whether Pocket Falls may set alarms. If the settings say Not allowed, turn alarms on for Pocket Falls in the Settings app. Please don't rely on the alarm alone for anything important.

VoiceOver

With VoiceOver on, the pond takes your touches directly, so you can stir the water and double-tap to feed the koi. A Settings button is always there for VoiceOver, and everything in the settings can be read and changed.

Language

Pocket Falls is in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). It follows your iPhone's language. To choose another just for Pocket Falls, go to Settings > Apps > Pocket Falls > Language.

Purchases and Refunds

Pocket Falls is a one-time purchase on the App Store, with no in-app purchases. It's yours on every iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Privacy

Pocket Falls collects no data. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.
